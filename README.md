One original one-line joke about office life, written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The meeting to cut unnecessary meetings ran two hours over, so we booked a follow-up.

The joke turns on itself at the end: a meeting held to cut meetings runs over, so the team books yet another meeting about it. Built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes".
